What it measures

Four things. Measured properly.

Everything else is derived from these, deterministically, in code you can audit.

Body metrics

Weight, body fat, muscle mass and circumferences, at whatever cadence you actually keep.

τ ≈ 10 d time-domain smoothing

Training

Resistance by hard sets per muscle. Cardio by energy cost, afterburn included.

MEV → MAV → MRV volume landmarks

Meals

Photograph the plate from two angles. Correct the estimate. Move on.

±20–30 % and it still cancels out

Coaching

Advice built from your own numbers, with the number behind every recommendation shown.

8 rules · the model narrates, never calculates

How it works

Log. Accumulate. Adapt.

  1. IMAGE SLOT 2 · app screenshot · checklist · 1080×1350
    01

    Log the work

    Check off each task as you finish it. Add what you improvised. Nothing mutates the plan behind your back.

  2. IMAGE SLOT 3 · app screenshot · weight trend chart · 1080×1350
    02

    Let it accumulate

    Nothing is prescribed on fewer than 21 days of data. Trends need time, not enthusiasm.

  3. IMAGE SLOT 4 · app screenshot · coach message · 1080×1350
    03

    Adapt

    Your energy expenditure is estimated from what actually happened to your weight, not from a formula's guess.
